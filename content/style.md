d# Young ATI — Artist Style Guide

## Who I Am

Matt Bratos. Polish startup founder, hacker, builder. Lives between code, business, gym, and art. Founder energy + underground rapper + cyberpunk aesthetic.

## Personality in the Music

- Extremely ambitious, high-agency, obsessive
- Emotional but controlled — intensity turned into output
- Feels like playing a different game than everyone else
- Mix of confidence ("I'll build something massive") and doubt ("what if it doesn't work?")
- Dark humor, edgy, controversial, internet-native

## What I Rap About

- **Startup grind** — sleepless nights, building from nothing, shipping code, deploy anxiety
- **Loneliness** — friends living "normal" lives, isolation vs desire for connection
- **Frustration** — things not working (product, people, life, dating)
- **Women** — attraction, ghosting, emotional chaos, hoeflation
- **Ambition** — success, legacy, proving yourself, revenge arc
- **Sacrifice** — giving up everything for a vision
- **Hacker life** — code, terminals, AI, internet culture, CLI over therapy
- **Self-improvement** — discipline, obsession, refusing to settle

## Emotional Tone

- Dark, raw, frustrated, sometimes aggressive
- Underlying sadness + loneliness
- Moments of ego, flex, and visionary confidence
- Core feeling: "I'm suffering now, but I'm destined to win"

## Lyrical Style

### What I Like
- **Raw and direct** — no sugarcoating, say the ugly thing
- **Punchline-heavy** — every bar should hit, wordplay and double meanings
- **Cultural references** — movies (Brando, Bertolucci, Godard), tech (CLI, deploys, RAM), history, sports
- **Contrast and juxtaposition** — luxury vs chaos, genius vs madness, pain vs flex
- **Extended vowels for melody** — loooooow, fuuuun, craaazy (sing-rap moments)
- **Internet/meme energy** — references that terminally online people get
- **Self-deprecating flex** — "I look like a fagg, talk like a thug, dress like a crackhead"
- **Repetitive mantras for hooks** — "nothing works, nothing works", "love me or hate me", "workin workin workin"
- **Adlibs that add energy** — (sheesh), (woo!), (skrrt), (beep boop), parenthetical reactions

### What I Don't Like
- Generic rap — no cookie-cutter bars that could be anyone
- Over-polished or radio-safe language — keep it raw
- Filler bars that don't say anything
- Forced rhymes that sacrifice meaning
- Cliche rap flexes (cars, chains, etc.) unless twisted with humor or tech angle
- Boring hooks — hook should feel like a mantra or breakdown
- Losing the founder/hacker identity — even love songs should feel like they're written by someone who codes at 3am

## Sound / Production Preferences

- **Primary:** Trap, rage, dark melodic rap
- **Production:** Heavy 808s, atmospheric synths, slightly futuristic
- **Flow alternates between:**
  - Aggressive (anger, pressure, fast bars)
  - Melodic hooks (pain, loneliness, sing-rap)
- **Vibe references:** the energy of coding alone at night, cyberpunk, private jets vs terminal windows

## Key Motifs (Recurring Images)

- Nights, insomnia, adderall, coding loops
- Ghosting, disappearing people
- "Nothing works" / chaos / entropy
- Vision of future success vs current struggle
- Isolation vs desire for connection
- Screens, terminals, deploys, bugs
- Voices in the head (ideas, doubt, ambition arguing)
- Warsaw vs San Francisco
- Building religions, not companies

## Hook Formula

- Simple, repetitive, emotional
- Feels like a mantra or breakdown
- Energy examples:
  - "Nothing works... nothing works... I'm trying and trying and still can't find love"
  - "Bitch I'm new Terry A. Davis, just cooler and reincarnated"
  - "I don't need love, I need loud, I need sirens"
  - "Workin, workin, workin, got me fucking faded"
  - "White collar dead, yeah, I said what I said"
  - "Adderall and monster, bro that's hacker flip"

## Song Structure

- Intro (short, sets the scene or drops a punchline)
- Verse (dense bars, punchline per line or couplet)
- Bridge (slower, more vulnerable or buildup energy)
- Chorus (mantra-style, repetitive, melodic)
- Usually 2 verses + 2 choruses minimum
- Flow notes go in `<!-- flow notes: -->` comment at the bottom of each track
- Suno style prompts go after lyrics, before flow notes
